Spotify listening dashboard

Turn your streaming history into something worth looking at.

Drop in your Spotify data export and this builds an interactive dashboard of your listening — top artists, how your taste shifted year to year, when you listen, and which artists stuck around. Everything runs in your browser.

1. Request your data from Spotify

Go to your Spotify Account → Privacy settings and request Extended streaming history. It's the richer export — the small "Account data" one won't go back as far. Spotify emails you a zip, usually within a few days.

2. Unzip it

You'll get a folder with files named like Streaming_History_Audio_2023_1.json. You may have several.

3. Drop them below

Select every Streaming_History_Audio file at once. Other files in the export are ignored.
